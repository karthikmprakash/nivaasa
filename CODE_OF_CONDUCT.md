# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nivaasa to be a place where people can contribute without harassment, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assigning motives.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's data, address, credentials or private conversation.
- Credit work that you build on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, community chat and any space where someone represents Nivaasa.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to **mkarthikprakash.work@gmail.com**. Include what happened, where, and who was involved. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participation, depending on severity and pattern.

We will respond as promptly as we can and will not retaliate against a good-faith reporter.
